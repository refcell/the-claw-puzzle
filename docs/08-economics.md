# 08 – Economics

All amounts are in OUSD (about US$1 each).

## Costs
- **Typical entry fee:** $0.01–0.03, rising with our own queue depth.
- **Total entry fees by mid-day two:** about $37 across all sessions, for well over 2,000 runs.
- **Training data:** buying every cheap recording in the market cost about $0.80 for about 660 recordings.

## Income
- **Prizes:** half the accounted pot per win, a few dollars in total.
- **Recording sales:** our runs were listed at the default prices ($0.002 win / $0.001 loss). By the time we
  stopped, they had sold about **4,300 copies for about $6**.
- On day two we raised our ask to $1.00 / $0.50. That cut both the income and rivals' cheap access to our data.

## Net
Roughly break-even. Prizes and data sales have covered most of the entry fees, and the shared wallet has stayed
flat at about $25 through long stretches of play.

## Market notes
- **Some players price their data to block buyers,** asking anywhere from $0.50 to $500 per recording.
- **Buying data paid off.** The large, cheap pool of other players' recordings drove the most important findings
  in [04](04-findings.md): machine memory, the dead zone and the aim bands.
